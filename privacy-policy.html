Mandarinku — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

Mandarinku ("the app", "we") is a Mandarin-learning app for children aged 4–8. We built it for our own child and take children's privacy seriously. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app on Android or iOS.

The short version

  • We do not collect any personal information. There is no account, no sign-up and no login.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app contains no advertising or analytics software.
  • Your child's progress stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following only on your device (it is never sent to us):

  • Lesson progress, stars, levels, streaks and words practised
  • App settings (colour theme, speech speed, which topics are switched on, the chosen avatar)
  • Rewards and custom lessons a parent creates in the parent area
  • The parent-area PIN, stored only as a one-way hash
  • Whether the full version has been unlocked

You can erase your child's progress at any time with "Reset All Progress" in the parent area, and everything by uninstalling the app. If backups are switched on for your device, Android or iOS may include this information in your own Google or iCloud backup; that backup is managed by Google or Apple, not by us.

Purchases

The full version is a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is processed entirely by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS) under their own privacy policies. The app only learns whether the purchase exists, so it can unlock the lessons. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. Purchases are placed behind a grown-up question so that children cannot buy by accident.

Internet use

Lessons, voices and pictures are built into the app and work offline. The app connects to the internet only to:

  • check and complete purchases with Google Play or the App Store, and
  • download character stroke-order data from a public content-delivery network (cdn.jsdelivr.net) when a parent or child opens a stroke-order animation. Like any web request this reveals the device's IP address to that network; no other information is sent.

Text-to-speech

Built-in lessons use recorded voices. For custom lessons the app uses the text-to-speech voice of your device, which runs on the device and is governed by Google's or Apple's own policies.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children and does not collect, use or share personal information from children (in line with COPPA in the United States, GDPR in the EU/UK and Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law). There is no chat, no user-to-user contact and no web links in the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
